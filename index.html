My MSc final projectMachine learning · Healthcare

Predictive Modelling of Maternal Health Risks

I compared Random Forest, XGBoost and a neural network using physiological indicators such as age, blood pressure, blood sugar, body temperature and heart rate. I cleaned and balanced the data with SMOTE, engineered Pulse Pressure and Risk-Age Ratio, and evaluated each model across the three risk classes. XGBoost performed best overall, although the project also showed me why accuracy alone is not enough for a healthcare problem.

04 / Visualisation

How I turn data into a useful visual.

This interactive example uses illustrative retail data. It demonstrates chart selection and communication, not confidential employer information.

TREND VIEW

Revenue and fulfilment over six months

Sample data

My approach

  1. Start with the decision.Clarify what the audience needs to compare, monitor or investigate.
  2. Choose the simplest visual.Use trends for time, bars for categories and scatter plots for relationships.
  3. Keep context visible.Label units, explain filters and distinguish real results from illustrative data.
  4. Highlight the takeaway.Use colour sparingly so attention goes to the insight rather than decoration.
