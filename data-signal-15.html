Before turning a headline into a conclusion

Check the denominator, geography, timetable and status: does the number describe an announcement, consultation or completed exit? An AI strategy mentioned beside a restructuring does not establish how many jobs AI replaced.

For a business review, I would track redeployment, workload, customer service and delivery quality alongside cost savings. Those outcomes are not measured here.

What this dataset can—and cannot—tell us

Seven plan components across six deliberately selected companies, disclosed in October–November 2026 and checked on 17 November. This is not an exhaustive tracker, a representative sample, a trend in economy-wide layoffs or a UK/European redundancy total. The scope is company-wide/global as reported.

Company statements establish what was disclosed, not independent proof of every explanation or completed exit. Announcement/event dates are separate from filing dates and exit timing. Unknown quantities stay blank. Percentages are neither summed nor averaged. No job count is inferred from a workforce percentage. Nearly 660 is displayed as a qualified rounded figure.

Imperial’s 4,200–4,600 programme range is calculated only from its two explicitly separate components: 2,100 + 2,100–2,500. Consultation is not completion. Charge and payment timetables do not establish exit completion dates.
