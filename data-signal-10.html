A record month for electric cars.
What would make you switch?

A visual snapshot of UK new-car registrations in September 2026. Prepared by Gokul Srinivasan.

The business question

What would convince more buyers to choose an electric car? Registrations show how the market changed, but do not establish the reasons behind individual purchases.

For a dealer, a useful next step would be to compare offer conversion and total ownership costs. For a charging operator, it would be to examine local demand alongside charging access. These are proposed questions for further analysis, not conclusions established by this dataset.

Method and scope

Year-on-year change = (2026 registrations ÷ 2025 registrations − 1) × 100. Shares and growth rates are rounded to one decimal place.

These are new registrations, not all cars on UK roads. September is a plate-change month; this single-month comparison is not a full-year forecast. Battery-electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles are separate categories. This is a static dashboard and published-data case study, not a live data feed.
