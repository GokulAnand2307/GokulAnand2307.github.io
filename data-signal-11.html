Read the signal carefully

These percentages measure reported concern, not actual delivery failures, lost revenue or probabilities of disruption. The supply-chain question asks about the next year and covers businesses with 10 or more employees. The energy comparison covers all sizes and a specific industry; the industry is a subset of the wider population. Do not add the two groups or compare them as independent samples.

Supply-chain factors can overlap, so their percentages should not be added. The two leading factors shown are not an exhaustive breakdown. The September 2025 supply-chain figure is derived as 48 − 17 = 31% from the rounded figures in the ONS release; it is not a separately downloaded precise estimate.

BICS is a voluntary survey and these are official statistics in development. Wave 165 ran from 21 September to 4 October 2026, with 9,933 responses and a 25.7% response rate. Estimates are subject to sampling and non-sampling uncertainty; confidence intervals are not plotted here. BICS does not cover all industries or the public sector. Unless otherwise specified, businesses are those that have not permanently stopped trading. This dashboard uses selected rounded headline estimates, not respondent-level records.
