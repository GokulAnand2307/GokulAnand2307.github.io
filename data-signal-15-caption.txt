A company can cut jobs and still grow its workforce.

That was the detail that stayed with me while putting this dashboard together.

For Gokul’s Data Signal 15, I looked across technology, IT services, finance and consumer businesses—including FAANG + NVIDIA.

The numbers tell different stories.

Amazon’s January update covered approximately 16,000 roles. Meta’s July call discussed around 8,000 employees affected by its May reduction. These are specific rounds, not this week’s totals.

Accenture reported severance costs, yet its workforce rose from approximately 779,000 to 814,000 over its financial year.

HSBC reported 4,390 fewer employee FTEs over nine months. That is a net workforce change—not automatically 4,390 redundancies.

And when a company’s reviewed disclosure does not provide a comparable layoff count, I’ve left it unavailable. Missing is not zero.

For me, the question goes beyond “who cut the most?”

Which roles are disappearing? Where is hiring continuing? And are businesses improving how work gets done, or passing more work to the people who remain?

I built an interactive dashboard so you can filter by industry, compare the different measures and inspect each company’s source and timeline.
